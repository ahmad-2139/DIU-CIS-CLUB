Who We Are

The CIS Club at Daffodil International University is a premier student organization dedicated to fostering a culture of technological innovation and leadership. We bridge the gap between academic learning and industry requirements.

Established with the vision to create a collaborative environment, we organize various events, seminars, and workshops throughout the year.

  • Student-led initiatives
  • Industry collaboration
  • Community support

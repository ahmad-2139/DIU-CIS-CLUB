Welcome to CIS Club

The CIS Club is the heartbeat of the Computing and Information System department at Daffodil International University. Since our inception, we have focused on one goal: Innovation through Collaboration.

We provide a platform for students to explore emerging technologies, participate in competitive programming, and engage in community services.

3rd Place: Summer 2025 Club Enrollment Campaign

The CIS Club demonstrated exceptional organizational prowess and community outreach during the Summer 2025 enrollment drive. Through strategic planning and enthusiastic engagement, the team secured the 3rd position overall, significantly expanding our member base and fostering a vibrant tech community on campus.

Technical Innovation

2nd Runner-Up: 4th Data Science Summit

Our talented team showcased their technical expertise in artificial intelligence at the prestigious 4th Data Science Summit AI Project Competition. Their innovative project secured the 2nd Runner-Up position among stiff competition, highlighting the department's strength in cutting-edge technology and problem-solving.

Individual Brilliance & IoT

Innovation in Public Safety: Metro Rail Monitoring System

CIS student Iran Sardar has developed a groundbreaking 'Metro Rail Bearing Pad Safety Monitoring System'. Utilizing advanced IoT sensors and robotics, this proactive solution instantly detects structural deviations or vibrations in bearing pads. The system triggers real-time alarms and alerts control rooms to prevent potential accidents, significantly enhancing national infrastructure safety for passengers.

About the Club

Our Mission

The mission of the CIS Club is to create a nurturing environment where students can bridge the gap between theoretical knowledge and practical application.

Our History

Established in Fall 2015, the CIS Club started as a small study group. Today, it is one of the most vibrant clubs at DIU, organizing national-level hackathons and tech fests.
